System requirements

AI video conversion is demanding. Check your PC before installing.

Recommended

  • Windows 10 / Windows 11
  • NVIDIA RTX GPU, 6 GB+ VRAM recommended
  • Recent NVIDIA driver
  • SSD recommended
  • VR headset, 3D projector or 3D TV for playback

Realistic expectations

The result is AI-generated stereoscopic 3D, not studio-authored native stereo footage. Quality depends on the source video, motion, compression, settings and playback device.

Use only video content you own or have rights to use. The software is not intended for DRM bypass, ripping, streaming capture, Blu-ray decryption or pirated content.

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers before you download.

Does Vistora AI 3D upload my video to the cloud?

No. Conversion is performed locally on your Windows PC.

What does the Free Trial include?

The Free Trial lets you test the main conversion workflow and supported output formats. Trial output is limited to up to 90 seconds and includes a watermark.

Which 3D formats are supported?

Vistora AI 3D supports Half-OU, Full-OU, Half-SBS and Full-SBS output.

What hardware is recommended?

Windows 10 or 11 with a modern NVIDIA GPU is recommended. An RTX-class GPU with 6 GB or more VRAM and an SSD will provide a better experience.

Is AI-generated 3D the same as native stereoscopic filming?

No. Vistora AI 3D estimates scene depth from a 2D source and synthesizes stereoscopic views. Results vary by scene and source quality.
